Sheet Logic Builder answers “how many sheets for this wall, floor, or roof face?” from dimensions you type — not from a cut list. It lays out standard 4×8-style panels on schematic diagrams for estimation and ordering. The Deck tab counts decking courses on a plan view (plus optional railing) — not solid lumber boards. Pair it with Sheet Logic when you have a cut list and need nest layouts on plywood or lumber.

Export to Offcut Logic

After you verify sizes on a tab, use Save this tab for Offcut… (toolbar, next to Print/PDF) to save a .wsoffcut.json file. Open it in Offcut Logic (separate app and license) to track rack stock and job parts.

Builder vs Sheet Logic

Sheet Logic Builder — quick surface-material estimates from length, width, height, pitch, and openings. Schematic diagrams for sales and field checks.

Sheet Logic — import a parts list, optimize plywood and solid lumber, print numbered nest layouts. Unlimited parts per job.

Many shops use Builder for rough-order quantities on additions and garages, and Sheet Logic for cabinet and millwork cut lists.

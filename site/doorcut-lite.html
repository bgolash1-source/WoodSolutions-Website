DoorCut Lite is the same DoorCut 3.0 installer with a streamlined edition for shops and hobbyists who run five-piece doors only — one cope-and-stick style with two stiles, two rails, and one raised or flat panel. Joinery math, machining allowances, and cut lists are built for that single door type; glass, muntins, arches, and the other Pro styles are not included.

What DoorCut Lite includes

Streamlined edition — same DoorCut 3.0 installer; your Lite license key unlocks this edition.

One door style: five-piece cope-and-stick (DoorCut style 1) — size the opening, set joinery and machining allowances, and produce stile, rail, and panel cut lists for the shop. Wood and sheet optimization are included for that cutlist. Lite does not include the additional Pro door styles (glass, arches, muntins, slab + edge band, and the rest).

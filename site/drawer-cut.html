DrawerCut

Drawer boxes and fronts with the same practical workflow as DoorCut — tuned for drawer banks, slide clearances, and production cut lists.

Planned focus

  • Drawer box sizing from opening dimensions and slide spec
  • Front overlay, reveal, and bank layout
  • Cut lists for box parts and drawer fronts
  • Materials library shared with the product line
  • Sheet Logic integration for bottoms and sheet parts

Status

DrawerCut follows DoorCut in the release schedule. Much of the domain logic lives in the same codebase family as CabinetLogic — this will be the standalone drawer shop tool when it ships.

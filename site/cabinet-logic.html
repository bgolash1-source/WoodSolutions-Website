CabinetLogic

The shop-floor hub for full casework — doors, drawers, face frames, and cabinet parts in one project cut list. Built on the same engine as DoorCut and DrawerCut, with 32 mm and face-frame workflows in active development.

Planned focus

  • Project-level cut lists — doors, drawers, and case parts together
  • Face-frame and frameless (32 mm) cabinet support
  • Shared door and drawer modules from DoorCut / DrawerCut
  • Sheet Logic for case goods and panel optimization
  • CAD Test sandbox for elevation and layout verification (internal R&D)

Status

CabinetLogic is the long-term integration target for the product line. DoorCut 3.0 ships first as a focused door-shop tool; CabinetLogic brings doors and drawers together with full cabinet context.

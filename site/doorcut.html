What DoorCut does

DoorCut calculates every part in a typical cabinet door — stiles, rails, panels, and muntins — with full control over machining allowances and panel placement within each style. Originally developed in Borland Delphi and now modernized in C#.

Door styles & sizing

Standard raised-panel, glass, and arched styles with definable stile, rail, and muntin widths. Custom panel placement lets you get new looks from the same base style.

Joinery math

Cope-and-stick and mortise-and-tenon modes with tenon and panel inset allowances calculated into every rail, stile, and muntin length.

Cut lists

Production-ready part lists for the table saw and CNC — quantities, dimensions, and material assignments in one job.

Materials library

Assign sheet goods and solid stock to frame and panel parts. Save your shop’s common materials for repeat jobs.

Label printing

Print labels for the shop floor so parts stay tied to the right door and opening through machining and assembly.

Sheet Logic (shared line)

Solid-wood and sheet-goods optimizers are rolling out through Sheet Logic — the same engine planned for DrawerCut and CabinetLogic.

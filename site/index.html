Built for the door shop

DoorCut calculates every part in a typical cabinet door — stiles, rails, panels, and muntins — with full control over machining allowances and panel placement within each style.

Originally developed in Borland Delphi and now modernized in C#, with sheet and solid-wood optimization on the way through Sheet Logic.

  • All stile, rail, and muntin widths definable
  • Custom panel placement — new looks from the same base style
  • Cut lists for the table saw and CNC
  • Label printing for the shop floor

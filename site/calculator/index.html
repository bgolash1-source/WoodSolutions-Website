First time installing an app from a website?

Quick Fraction Calculator is not on Google Play — you download it directly from woodsolutions.com, the same way many independent shop apps are sold. These steps assume you have never sideloaded an app before.

  1. On your phone or tablet, open this page in Chrome (bookmark it — do not use random APK sites from a web search).
  2. Tap Download for Android above — wait until the download finishes (Wi‑Fi helps on slower connections).
  3. Open your downloads: pull down the notification and tap the file, or in Chrome tap ⋮ (menu) → Downloads → QuickFractionCalculator-Android-1.0.apk.
  4. If Android asks to Allow install from this source, turn it on for Chrome or Files — this is a one-time permission for apps not from the Play Store.
  5. If Google Play Protect warns you, tap More details → Install anyway (only when you downloaded from this page).
  6. Tap Install, then Open. On the purple screen tap Continue to app to start your 14-day trial — no purchase needed yet.

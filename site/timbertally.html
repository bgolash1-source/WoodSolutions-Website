TimberTally

A free Wood Solutions tool for board-foot calculations and lumber costing — practical math for the lumber pile, not the sales desk.

What it does

  • Board-foot volume from thickness, width, and length
  • Lumber cost estimates from your price per board foot
  • Cut optimization helpers for shop planning
  • Windows desktop app — self-contained installer, no separate .NET download

Download

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Download the zip, extract, and run the setup inside. No license key — TimberTally is freeware for personal or commercial shop use. Redistribution and resale are not permitted.

Installer size is about 55 MB (compressed zip on the server).

Download TimberTallySetup-1.0.0.zip
